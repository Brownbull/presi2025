# Marketing Skill for Khujta AI - Análisis Presidencial 2025

## Purpose
This skill helps create marketing content, identify target audiences, generate social media posts, and suggest site improvements to make the Chilean Presidential Analysis project more appealing and reach a wider audience.

### 4. Campaign Strategy
- Launch campaigns

## Project Context

**Site:** https://brownbull.github.io/presi2025/
**Purpose:** AI-powered analysis of 8 Chilean presidential programs from 28 electoral personas
**Key Stats:** 1,547 measures analyzed, 224 evaluations, vibecoded in 48 hours
**Tech Stack:** Eleventy, Claude Sonnet 4.5, GitHub Pages
**Branding:** Khujta AI - independent, transparent, civic tech

## Target Audiences

1. **Undecided Voters** (HIGH PRIORITY)
   - Demographics: 25-55, middle class, urban
   - Pain Point: Overwhelmed by campaign rhetoric, no time to read programs
   - Message: "Find your candidate in 5 minutes based on YOUR reality"

2. **Politically Engaged Citizens**
   - Demographics: 30-65, active on social media
   - Pain Point: Need data for discussions, want to fact-check
   - Message: "Data-driven analysis. 1,547 measures cited. Share evidence, not opinions."

3. **Tech-Savvy Millennials & Gen Z**
   - Demographics: 18-35, digital natives
   - Pain Point: Politics is boring, don't trust traditional media
   - Message: "AI analyzing Chilean politics. The future of electoral info is here."

4. **Educators & Students**
   - Demographics: Teachers, professors, students
   - Pain Point: Need structured data for teaching/research
   - Message: "Open methodology, structured data, perfect for civic education."
